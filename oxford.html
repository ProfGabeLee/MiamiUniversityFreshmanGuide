Welcome to Oxford, Ohio.

Steps from Miami University's red-brick quads, Uptown Oxford blends brick-lined streets, local eateries, wooded trails, and welcoming hotels for visiting families.

location_city
THINGS TO DO

Local attractions worth exploring.

Whether you are taking a study break or showing visiting family around Butler County, Oxford has plenty to see within minutes of campus.

forest Outdoors · 10 min drive

Hueston Woods State Park

Nearly 3,000 acres of hiking trails, Acton Lake canoeing, fossil hunting, and a historic pioneer homestead.

storefront Walkable · High Street

Uptown Historic District

Brick sidewalks lined with local boutiques, coffee shops, Uptown Park concerts, and the Saturday Farmers Market.

palette Arts · 10 S. College Ave

Oxford Community Arts Center

A restored historic building hosting rotating art galleries, live performances, workshops, and community festivals.

directions_bike Trails · Corso Road

Black Covered Bridge & Trails

Follow the paved Oxford Area Trails out to this 19th-century wooden covered bridge and scenic creek overlook.

museum History · 401 E. Spring St

McGuffey House & Museum

National Historic Landmark where William Holmes McGuffey compiled his famous 19th-century school readers.

yard Nature · Campus Edge

Conrad Formal Gardens

Quiet landscaped gardens and walking paths right beside the Marcum Hotel—ideal for photos and afternoon walks.

UPTOWN DINING

Favorite Oxford restaurants.

From quick steamed bagels between classes to sit-down family dinners on Move-In Weekend, these local staples define eating in Oxford.

bakery_dining
High Street · Iconic

Bagel & Deli Shop

An Oxford staple since 1975 featuring 90+ creative steamed bagel sandwiches right across from campus.

lunch_dining
11 W. High St · Deli

Bodega Delicatessen

Hand-crafted sandwiches, house soups, and fresh deli salads—an easy lunch stop during campus visits.

local_pizza
31 E. High St · Pizza

Bruno's Pizza

Serving Uptown since 1963 with classic pies, slices, subs, and signature garlic Bruno Dough.

dinner_dining
308 S. Campus Ave · Italian

Paesano's Pasta House

Cozy family-run restaurant known for build-your-own scratch pasta plates—arrive early on busy weekends.

restaurant
15 E. High St · Bavarian

Steinkeller

Warm underground bier hall serving soft pretzels with house bier cheese, schnitzel, and hearty German dishes.

brunch_dining
325 S. College Ave · American

Gaslight Brewhouse

Scratch kitchen off campus offering burgers, sandwiches, and a relaxed setting popular with visiting parents.
